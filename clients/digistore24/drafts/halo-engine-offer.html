The Problem

Digistore24's growth engine is judged on signups, but the business runs on activated vendors — and activation lags acquisition by ~3 months. Today every channel is graded by its own platform's numbers, which means no two channels can be compared on what actually matters: cost per activated vendor and CLTV per dollar.

Why It Exists

This is not a data problem — the events exist in PostHog. It is an identity and instrumentation problem, in four specific places: click IDs die at funnel handoffs (URL-param capture loses ~30% even post-fix; cookie-read closes it); vendor records carry no acquisition fields; revenue events don't reliably join back to signup identities (today only 20 of 93 first-sale vendors match a purchase record); and nothing feeds activation truth back into platform bidding. Fix those four joints and every channel becomes measurable on the same spine — click → signup → product created → first sale → ongoing GMV.

The Outcome

  • Within 60 days: capture and identity fixed across all funnels (Google, Microsoft, Meta, affiliate click IDs); revenue join resolved; the live activation tracker generalized from one channel to all of them.
  • Within 2 quarters: a channel league table — spend → signups → activation rate → CLTV per dollar — updated daily, plus activation-weighted conversions feeding every platform's bidding.
  • Ongoing: budget allocation decisions made on measured CLTV-per-dollar, with incrementality (halo) analysis showing what each channel lifts beyond what its platform claims.

The Framework — Five Steps

STEP 1
Capture
Click IDs (gclid, msclkid, fbclid, affiliate IDs) + UTM discipline across every funnel — cookie-read, not URL-param, so nothing dies at handoffs.
STEP 2
Identify
Acquisition fields written onto the vendor record at signup; revenue events joined to vendor identity — one spine per vendor.
STEP 3
Cohort
Weekly signup cohorts per channel with activation measured at fixed ages (7/14/30/60/90 days) — cohorts compare honestly as they mature.
STEP 4
Compare
The league table: CAC → activation → CLTV per dollar by channel, plus halo/incrementality testing across channels.
STEP 5
Feed & Govern
Offline activation conversions uploaded to every ad platform so all bidding optimizes activations; quarterly measurement review for budget allocation.
