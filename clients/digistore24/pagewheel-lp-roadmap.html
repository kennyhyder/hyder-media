PageWheel — Landing Page Roadmap

Where dedicated landing pages will lift Quality Score and conversion rate, ranked from live campaign data. Today every ad group routes to the single /pagewheel-ai-page-builder page, which leads with "sales pages & funnels" — every theme whose searcher wanted something else pays a QS tax as higher CPCs and suppressed impression share (most ad groups serve at only 13–28% of eligible impressions).

Built from the Aug 3 search-term audit · 14-day campaign data ($6.8K spend, 3,394 visible queries) + Google Keyword Planner volumes · Conversion counts carry the tracking caveat below

The Play

Ship Ebook + Digital Product Creator pages first — they cover $3.8K of the $6.8K fortnightly spend.

Both themes already show real engagement (6.8–8.9% CTR) and the campaign's only conversion signal, but sit at QS 2–5 because the destination page never speaks their language. QS relief on these two lowers CPCs on the majority of the budget. The done-for-you/PLR page is the cheapest high-intent test on the list. Builder and funnel pages are volume plays to sequence after the first wave proves out.

Standing caveat: the only conversion action live is "PageWheel Purchase" (deep-funnel, $0 value, cross-domain attribution unverified). All conversion-rate judgments inherit that gap — trial-start tracking remains pre-work for reading any of these pages' true results.
