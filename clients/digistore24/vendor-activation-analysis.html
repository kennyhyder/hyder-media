Coverage caveat — the one question for the DS24 data team
Only 20 of 93 activated vendors match purchase records, and matches are EUR-only. Either the PostHog purchase event doesn't cover all order forms/currencies (likely, given US campaigns sell in USD), or vendor IDs only partially join. All GMV figures here are floors. Question to resolve: does Purchase Completed cover all checkout types and currencies, and is First Sale.userId the same identifier as Purchase Completed.vendor_id?
